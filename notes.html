Why Egyptian Arabic?

Egyptian Arabic is the dialect you hear in Egyptian movies, music, and everyday conversation, and it is a little different from the formal Arabic used in writing and news. I want to be able to follow a conversation and join in on the jokes. My favorite word so far is yalla, which people use to say let's go.

Pronunciation tips
  • Say the sounds out loud, even when it feels silly.
  • Arabic is written from right to left, so the first letter is on the right.
  • Listen to native speakers and copy their rhythm.

I am still learning, so double-check anything important with a fluent speaker.
